Scope a study. Check the venue.

This page does two things, and it decides them differently. Study proposals come from a model, because whether a question is worth asking and can be finished in the weeks you have is judgement. The venue verdict is a database lookup with the matched record shown, because a model must never be the thing that tells you a conference is real. It will answer UNKNOWN when it cannot find a record, which happens often on conferences. That is the honest answer rather than a guess, and here is the measurement of how often.

Did it get something wrong?

That is the useful case and I would rather hear about it than not. If it refused a venue you know is legitimate, or returned a record for one you know is not, email me at 20hozaifa02@gmail.com with the name you typed. The wrong answers are what the index gets fixed from. You can also read what decides what here, and how often it refuses.

Benchless teaches the method and checks the work. It does not write, submit, or co-author anything. What you send to a venue is yours, and you are responsible for it. Venue verdicts are lookups against public indexing records, shown with their source so you can verify them yourself. They are not endorsements, and an indexed result is not advice to submit.
